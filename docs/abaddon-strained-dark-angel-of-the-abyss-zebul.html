Abaddon is one of the greatest protectors you may have at your side. He can wreak havoc upon enemies, obstacles, weaknesses, chains and anything which may seek to stop you. He will reveal the path ahead of and no matter how confusing it may be, he will lead you to make sense of it all. His Abyssal Fire destroys all that it touches and through this you will move forward unassailed. The utterly destructive forces he wields can be terrifying to witness yet equally as comforting when they are on your side defending you. If someone tries to control you, stands in your way or attacks you spiritually, you will witness how this terrifying fire appears as your greatest saviour.

Abaddon is the gatekeeper that stands between you and some of the most chaotic, horrific and destructive forces and powers that exist. Whether you want him as a guide, protector or if you wish to experience his judgement, be clear with your intentions.
