The original description can be found below.

This Ritualistic Subliminal is a work for attaining contact, connection and eventual merging with your Holy Guardian Angel. The HGA is a personal Spirit that was created at the exact same time as you. Everyone has their own personal one regardless of the path they walk and the HGA watches over them and knows everything about them, every person only has one.

The HGA is not a normal spirit in any way, it can be seen as an embodiment of the Divine (or Source, Absolute, or whatever you wish to call it) or perhaps like your "copy" or "twin" that is created of pure divinity, it is both you and not you at the same time. It is more than a "Perfected Self" and it is representative of your own truest divine nature. It is also your connection to the Divine. The term Divine here is not meant to refer to any belief systems or ideals, do not conflate it with this. It is not really an angel or demon despite the name, it is much more than that. It is a cosmic, divine and very powerful being. You should not confuse it with what people call a "guardian angel" these days because it is entirely different.

The original Abramelin operation for connecting to the HGA involved many months of effort and was an arduous process. It has been said by Aleister Crowley that "It should never be forgotten for a single moment that the central and essential work of the Magician is the attainment of the Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel."

By connecting to the HGA, you gain a lot. It is the best protector and guide you can have. It acts as an intermediary whenever you contact spirits and grants you power and authority. It can summon and control spirits for you. A person who has attained the connection with their HGA will be able to command spirits. You could ask your HGA to summon chosen spirits for you and give them your commands/requests. It grants you immense spiritual power and your magick will become much more powerful than you can imagine, and far more successful too. It grants you conscious realisation of your true, complete Self and the All.

The HGA helps you realize and enact your True Will. Everything becomes better and you gain power. This is not to say that life will never have a problem again but you will have less problems and a great advantage even throughout any problems. This connection will change your life a lot. The process is unique for each person but life will certainly be very different after in a good way. You may notice things you didn't before or gain new understanding that changes your life trajectory and even you as a person. No matter how badly you mess up in your magick or in your life, the HGA can help things go smoothly and prevent the worst outcomes.

You may notice that once this connection has been attained, your interactions with spirits are much easier and they are much easier and nicer to work with. This happens because the HGA holds great power and asserts your authority, it is essentially doing a lot of the work for you in the interactions. It can ensure the right spirit receives your request and not an impostor or anything else.

You may ask the HGA for help with anything or whatever you want. It is the being that is closest to you, and perhaps the only being that always has your best interests in mind and maintains good intentions towards you no matter the situation. It will help you regardless of your path and of what you want. It does behave as an advisor and guide but it will not force anything onto you. It will grant you insight and discernment so that you make the right choices but it will not make the choices for you.

The most important thing to attaining this connection is sincerity and intention. Be open to the connection. You may not immediately make contact with it but it is always there, and when the time is right you will be able to. This work will help you do so. Much more can be said but this is a topic of great debate, I suggest you do further research yourself and draw your own conclusions.

Besides the main ritualistic work, many subliminal affirmations and incantations are included to support and aid all of this work further.
