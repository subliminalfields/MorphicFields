Conceptual thinking is the ability to understand and organize complex ideas, concepts, and information. Creativity: The ability to generate new ideas and solutions to problems.

Critical thinking: The ability to evaluate information and make sound judgments.

Metacognition: The ability to think about one's own thinking and learn how to learn.
