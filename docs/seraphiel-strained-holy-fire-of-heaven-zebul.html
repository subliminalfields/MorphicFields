This Ritualistic Subliminal is a work for developing a deep connection and affinity with the Angel Seraphiel and calling forth his presence, blessing, aid, and support into your life.

Seraphiel is the chief of the Seraphim and is the highest ranked among them. He is of the Princes of the Merkabah and is also known to be the Protector of Metatron.

From his descriptions he is called a prince and described as wonderful, noble, great, honourable, mighty, terrible, a leader, a chief, a swift scribe, glorified, honoured and beloved. Much of his descriptions talk about splendour and a countenance of light, beauty and greatness. It is described that his height is "as the seven heavens".

It has been said the crown on his head is so great that there is no kind of splendour, brilliance, radiance or light that is not fixed upon it, and that this Crown is named the "Prince of Peace". It is also said that as he presides over the Seraphim he teaches them song, praise, proclamation of beauty, might and majesty all day and all night. The Seraphim and especially Seraphiel are associated with unimaginably great passion, dedication, devotion and faith. He is also associated with extreme "devekut" as it is known in the kabbalistic tradition.

The Seraphim are exceptionally powerful. They are associated with powerful light that is impossible to behold and pure fire that can destroy anything. In the Hierarchy of Angels, most systems place the Seraphim are at the very top while some others place them very high up.

Seraphiel is able to aid in all types of purification and cleansing. He can get rid of all negative and harmful forces and energies from within you and around you. He can deliver you from any form of attack and protect you during any form of spiritual warfare.

Whatever worries or troubles you have, you may call upon Seraphiel to soothe you and grant you relief.

Seraphiel may be called upon for any type of clearing or sanctification. You may call upon him to clear yourself, to clear and sanctify an altar to prepare it for use, or for any other reason or thing. You may call upon him in this manner for any object, item, person, or thing in any other way and once it is done, you will notice that it remains sanctified and protected. The Sanctification does not have to be done in name of any specific deity so it remains a good choice for any purpose and practitioner.

In the same manner, he may also get rid of any deserving target with ease. If you wish for something to be "burned away", whether figuratively or literally, then he is the one to call upon. This power may be used in any manner whether it is to burn away spiritual forces against you, physical things, certain memories of others, internal issues, or any manner of other things.

In this way too he can grant you true bliss and peace by clearing away all within you that does not serve you. He is able to get rid of all manner of internal issues such as anxiety.

Seraphiel is able to grant mystical knowledge and awareness upon any aspirant and foster growth and progression on their path. It does not matter whether you prefer the light or dark or have a balance, you may call upon him and he will help in a manner both befitting his nature and suitable to you. So too can he help beyond the mystical and he can grant you all the passion, dedication, devotion and faith you desire to whatever you wish, even just yourself.

If there are any forces working against you, whether spiritual or otherwise, you should call upon him and by doing so you will notice how quickly they either flee or are destroyed.

He may help with much beyond this too, remember that he is chief among some of the highest of angels so do not be afraid to make other requests of him. I have only described some of what he can help with.

Besides the main ritualistic work, many subliminal affirmations and incantations are included to support and aid all of this work further.

There are no restrictions on this work, you are in control. Stop fearing your Power, Control and Will and seize and exercise them.
