Mullein (Verbascum thapsus)

The Luminous Lungkeeper

A tall sentinel of soft, silver leaves and golden flowers, Mullein is a herb of deep comfort and structural support.

Soothes dry, irritated lungs

Acts as a gentle expectorant, loosening trapped mucus

Reduces inflammation and calms spasmodic coughs

Supports the respiratory tract in cases of asthma, bronchitis, and smoke exposure

Energetic & Spiritual Resonance:

Mullein is like an anchor of breath—drawing light into the lungs and grounding it.

It helps clear grief, sorrow, and emotional fog held in the chest.

Reconnects the breath with divine rhythm, acting as a “pillar of air” through the subtle body.

Strengthens the lung meridian in Traditional Chinese Medicine, which governs boundaries and the ability to receive.

Elecampane (Inula helenium)

The Deep Root of the Breath

Rich, earthy, and warm, Elecampane works at the deeper layers of lung health, penetrating into the roots of chronic congestion.

Powerful expectorant: helps expel thick, stuck mucus from the lungs

Anti-microbial and anti-fungal action for infections

Tones and strengthens weak lungs, particularly after long illness

Supports digestion, which in turn nourishes lung energy (Earth feeds Metal in TCM)

Energetic & Spiritual Resonance:

Elecampane is the “Root of Remembering” the full breath—inviting deep inhales and presence.

It works on ancestral imprints and inherited grief stored in the lungs.

Clears stagnation from old emotional patterns and karmic breath-holding.

Its energy is grounding yet ascending—bringing strength from the depths upward to feed the breath.

Thyme (Thymus vulgaris)

The Flame of Vital Breath

A protector herb with ancient lineage, Thyme kindles inner fire and strengthens the immune matrix of the lungs.

Antibacterial, antiviral, antifungal—perfect for clearing infection from the respiratory tract

Relieves tightness in the chest and opens bronchial pathways

Excellent for dry coughs, whooping cough, or early-stage cold and flu

Stimulates circulation, helping oxygenation reach deep tissues

Thyme brings courage—literally from the Greek thymos, meaning spirit, breath, or soul.

Clears stagnant fear and repressed expression from the chest and throat

Invites the breath to move with vitality, confidence, and flow

Acts as a purifier of the air element in the body, banishing stagnant energies and psychic residue

Thyme purifies and strengthens
