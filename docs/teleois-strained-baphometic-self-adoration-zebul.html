This Ritualistic Subliminal is a work based upon a full rite of Baphometic Self Adoration or Self-Prayer through Baphomet.

Baphomet is the symbol of ultimate balance, the ultimate and complete realisation of potential. It is perfectly representative of union of and mastery over all forces. This is why it can be seen as symbolic of and act as a doorway to the Absolute. It can even seen as a symbol of your own ultimate potential.

Because of what Baphomet is and represents, it can be worked with for absolutely anything. It can be worked with to achieve your highest potential in every way.

The rite which this work is based upon is an Adoration praising Baphomet as the Symbol of Perfection, the master alchemist, ultimate balance, master of all heavens and hells, the master of all elements and planets, the master of all forces of dark and light, the doorway to the perfection and the Absolute, and the symbolic representation of the Absolute itself. It recognises the absolute potential that Baphomet represents and the truly perfect balanced state of Baphomet. Then, it recognises and asserts that you yourself are Baphomet and Baphomet is you manifest as symbol in your highest form and ultimate potential, perfectly balanced in every manner and way.

Once Baphomet is recognised as yourself in highest form, the rite calls upon it to manifest in specific ways in your life. This is symbolic of asking your highest self, having realised its ultimate potential, for these things.

It asks for perfect balance to be attained in all areas of life. This means that all areas must flourish and blossom with equal force. If you are someone who considers yourself successful spiritually but in the physical world you have nothing to show for yourself, then this is not success because there is no balance. These seemingly opposing areas must be balanced and have harmony union and not seen as opposition, the success of each one may improve the other further. In this case, the Baphomet will lead you to attain equal success in the physical world, and you will keep progressing further in both. Similarly, if you are a person who only focuses on either your light or your darkness and resists or rejects the other, then this is not balance either. You must be able to work with both forces equally and see them as supportive to each other rather than opposing, or you will be forever caught in the middle of a self-created struggle. These are only two such examples, but it will apply to every single area of existence. Whether it is emotions, your mind or whatever else, balance will be found. All forces within you shall be perfectly balanced as shall all forces external to you.

Baphomet is also symbolic of the alchemist who, having attained complete mastery, fashioned itself into the ultimate symbol of alchemy. For this reason, there is nothing that the Baphomet fears whether it is pain or joy, negativity or positivity, or failure or success because it knows that they are more similar than they are different and one can easily be turned into the other. For this reason its alchemy is called upon. All forces that are against you, harmful, or otherwise in your way on the pursuit to perfection are turned into something greater. These can be internal forces such as your own beliefs and actions or these can be external forces such as the influence of planets or even curses and attacks. These do not even need to be ultimately or entirely negative. So long as they have a more favourable manifestation for you, it will be made such.

The Baphomet is called to make your entire day perfectly balanced so that every moment will bring with it perfection and success and be utilised correctly. Every moment of the day will be made to be the best it can be to further you on the path to perfection. You will find great focus and success in your work and you will find great replenishment in your rest. You shall find the strength, fortitude, energy and direction to do all you wish and attain all you seek. Your every act will be one that is effective and correct to further you on your path and each one shall be intentional. All you do shall abundantly bear the best fruits for you, and Baphomet is asked to give you the treasures of the earth, the hells and the heavens. Much more is in this part, all of it directed to perfecting the day. Because it is based upon perfecting each individual day, it should be used daily, as soon as possible after waking, and at least once for best results.

There is a great deal more that could be said about this work. It may help with absolutely everything. Just as Baphomet is the "Mystery of Mysteries", it is also the dispeller of mysteries and knower of all truths and lies. It is up to you to use it for yourself and see.

Remember. All You Wish For Is Yours. Simply Waiting For You To Take It. Be Fearless Son Of Man And Use The Secrets Of The Serpents.
